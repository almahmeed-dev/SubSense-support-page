Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SubSense ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our iOS app.

Information We Collect

  • Your subscription list: Names, prices, renewal dates, categories, notes, and preferences are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, Apple may sync this app data between your devices under your iCloud account.
  • Purchases and technical information: Apple processes Premium purchases. SubSense sends purchase records to RevenueCat for purchase-related app functionality and analytics for SubSense Premium purchases and renewals. RevenueCat uses an automatically generated app user identifier. Its SDK also sends Apple’s identifier for vendor (a device identifier), device model, app and operating-system versions, language settings, and App Store country information. SubSense does not send the name or email you enter in your local profile to RevenueCat.
  • Apple Ads attribution: SubSense enables Apple AdServices attribution through RevenueCat to measure Apple Ads acquisition and related subscription revenue. The app sends an attribution token to RevenueCat, which can request campaign attribution information from Apple. This processing is separate from app-usage diagnostics.
  • App-usage diagnostics: SubSense keeps usage-event counts and a limited queue of diagnostic events on your device to help improve flows such as onboarding and purchasing. Records include app actions, event times, a session identifier, app version, and language and region settings. These diagnostics are separate from purchase processing and Apple Ads attribution.
  • Exchange rates: SubSense requests exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API. These requests do not include your subscription list, amounts, name, or email. The provider receives ordinary network information such as your IP address and request details. See ExchangeRate-API’s privacy terms.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve app functionality.
  • To verify in-app purchases, restore Premium access, analyze SubSense Premium purchases and renewals, and measure Apple Ads performance.
  • To store your preferences and subscription data locally and, when enabled, sync them through iCloud.

Sharing of Information

Apple processes App Store purchases and iCloud sync. RevenueCat receives the purchase, technical, and attribution information described above. Your tracked subscription list is not sent to RevenueCat or ExchangeRate-API. If you export or share files, or add records to Calendar or Reminders, the information is handled by the apps, services, or accounts you choose. If you email support, we receive the contact information and message you choose to send.

Your Choices

You can delete subscription records in the app and manage iCloud for SubSense in iOS Settings. App-usage diagnostic preferences do not control Apple or RevenueCat purchase processing or Apple Ads attribution. Deleting tracking records does not cancel paid services, erase Apple or RevenueCat purchase records, or delete copies you saved or shared outside the app. Manage App Store subscriptions in your Apple Account subscription settings, and contact us below about privacy requests.

Children’s Privacy

SubSense does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove the information.
