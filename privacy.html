Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SubSense ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our iOS app.

Information We Collect

  • Your subscription list: Names, prices, renewal dates, categories, notes, and preferences are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, Apple may sync this app data between your devices under your iCloud account.
  • Purchase history: Purchases are processed by Apple. SubSense uses RevenueCat to verify subscription status and keep Premium access in sync. This purchase information is used only for app functionality, is not used for tracking, and is not linked by us to your identity.
  • App activity: SubSense keeps simple event counters on your device to help improve flows such as onboarding and purchasing. These counters are not sent to a third-party analytics service.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve app functionality.
  • To verify in-app purchases and restore Premium access.
  • To store your preferences and subscription data locally and, when enabled, sync them through iCloud.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking. Apple processes App Store purchases and iCloud sync. RevenueCat processes purchase history solely to provide and restore subscription access. Your subscription list is not sent to RevenueCat.

Your Choices

You can delete subscription records in the app, turn off iCloud for SubSense in iOS Settings, or remove the app to delete locally stored data. App Store subscriptions can be managed in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Children’s Privacy

SubSense does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove the information.
