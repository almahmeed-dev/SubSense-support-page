Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SubSense ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our iOS app. It covers the version on the App Store today and version 5.4, the update that follows it. Where something applies only from a certain version, the text says so.

Information We Collect

  • Your subscription list: Names, prices, renewal dates, categories, notes, and preferences are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, Apple may sync this app data between your devices through iCloud key-value storage under your iCloud account. We cannot read it.
  • Purchases and technical information: Apple processes Premium purchases. SubSense sends purchase records to RevenueCat for purchase-related app functionality and analytics for SubSense Premium purchases and renewals. The RevenueCat SDK starts when SubSense opens, so RevenueCat can receive the information in this item and the next one even if you have not bought Premium. RevenueCat uses an automatically generated app user identifier. Its SDK also sends Apple’s identifier for vendor (a device identifier), device model, app and operating-system versions, language settings, and App Store country information. SubSense does not send the name or email you enter in your local profile to RevenueCat.
  • Apple Ads attribution: SubSense enables Apple AdServices attribution through RevenueCat to measure Apple Ads acquisition and related subscription revenue. The app sends an attribution token to RevenueCat, which can request campaign attribution information from Apple. This processing is separate from app-usage diagnostics.
  • App-usage diagnostics: SubSense keeps usage-event counts and a limited queue of diagnostic events on your device to help improve flows such as onboarding and purchasing. Records include app actions, event times, a session identifier, app version, and language and region settings. From version 5.4 these records stay on your device and are not sent to a server. They are separate from purchase processing and Apple Ads attribution.
  • Exchange rates: SubSense’s screens, widgets, and shortcuts do not request exchange rates. The app still contains a currency-conversion service that can contact ExchangeRate-API, but no screen uses it. If a version ever does, that request would not include your subscription list, amounts, name, or email, and the provider would receive ordinary network information such as your IP address. See ExchangeRate-API’s privacy terms.

What SubSense Does on Your Device (Version 5.4 and Later)

  • Fill from a Receipt or Email: When you choose a screenshot with the system photo picker, paste text, or type text, SubSense reads it on your device. Apple’s text recognition turns a picture into text, then SubSense’s own rules read the text. If your iPhone supports it and Apple Intelligence is turned on, Apple’s on-device language model also reads a copy of the text with details such as email addresses, card and account numbers, and street addresses hidden first. The picture and text are not saved, uploaded, or sent to us or anyone else, and are dropped when the import screen closes. The result only fills the New Subscription form, and it is saved only if you tap Add. The system photo picker gives SubSense only the picture you choose, not your photo library.
  • Widgets and the Control Center button: After each change to your list, SubSense saves a small copy of your active subscriptions (name, price, currency, category symbol, next renewal date, billing cycle, and a monthly estimate) in an App Group, a storage area on your device shared only by SubSense and its widgets. Widgets read that copy to show renewals and totals. The widget’s show/hide-amounts choice is kept there too. Cancelled plans are not copied. This copy stays on your device.
  • Siri, Shortcuts, and Spotlight: Shortcuts and Siri requests that read or change your list run inside SubSense on your device. Your subscriptions (name, price, billing cycle, next renewal, and category) are added to your iPhone’s Spotlight index so you can search for them. The index stays on your device and does not include your notes. Siri and Spotlight are Apple features, and Apple’s own privacy terms cover how they handle your requests.
  • Install history check: To decide whether the free plan holds 5 or 7 subscriptions, SubSense asks the App Store, through Apple’s StoreKit, for the record of your original download. The record contains the first app version you installed, the date, whether it came from the App Store, TestFlight, or a test environment, whether it was refunded, and Apple’s transaction identifier. Apple may contact its servers to verify the record, as it does for purchases. SubSense also looks once for data from earlier versions of SubSense on your device and in your iCloud copy of the list. It keeps the result, and the date SubSense first ran on your iPhone, in the Keychain as items that stay on this device (they are not part of iCloud Keychain or device backups) and in the app’s preferences, so the answer holds if you delete and reinstall the app. These items can remain on your device after you delete SubSense. They are not sent to us, to RevenueCat, or to anyone else, and are not used to track you. If the App Store cannot be reached, SubSense uses what is on your device.
  • Tips and reminders: SubSense keeps a record on your device of which in-app tips it has shown and on which days you opened it. Renewal reminders are notifications scheduled on your device, and SubSense asks for permission only when you choose to turn them on.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve app functionality.
  • To verify in-app purchases, restore Premium access, analyze SubSense Premium purchases and renewals, and measure Apple Ads performance.
  • To store your preferences and subscription data locally and, when enabled, sync them through iCloud.
  • From version 5.4, to work out your free plan from your install history, as described above.

Sharing of Information

Apple processes App Store purchases, iCloud sync, and the install history record. RevenueCat receives the purchase, technical, and attribution information described above. Your tracked subscription list, and anything you read with Fill from a Receipt or Email, is not sent to RevenueCat or ExchangeRate-API. If you export or share files, or add records to Calendar or Reminders, the information is handled by the apps, services, or accounts you choose. If you email support, we receive the contact information and message you choose to send.

Your Choices

You can delete subscription records in the app and manage iCloud for SubSense in iOS Settings. You can remove SubSense widgets from your Home Screen or Lock Screen, and manage what SubSense shows in search and Siri suggestions in iOS Settings under SubSense and Siri & Search. App-usage diagnostic preferences do not control Apple or RevenueCat purchase processing or Apple Ads attribution. Deleting tracking records does not cancel paid services, erase Apple or RevenueCat purchase records, or delete copies you saved or shared outside the app. Deleting the app removes its preferences, the widget copy, and the Spotlight entries, but not the device-only Keychain items described under the install history check. Manage App Store subscriptions in your Apple Account subscription settings, and contact us below about privacy requests.

Children’s Privacy

SubSense does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove the information.
